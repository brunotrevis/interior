The Interior Index

How we choose a city

  1. A lake you can walk to

    Water in the middle of the city, ringed by a path anyone can use, at any hour.

  2. A centre that still has a square

    Somewhere to sit without buying anything, and someone to sit next to.

  3. Weather with opinions

    Seasons strong enough to give the year a shape and the calendar its festivals.

  4. A ritual shared by strangers

    A gourd of mate, a dance night, a Sunday in the grass. Habits that make a crowd into a circle.

  5. Work that does not need the capital

    Industry, farming, universities and services that let people stay.
